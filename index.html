Why We Exist

Europe's best talent. Finance's weakest bridge.

In the UK and US, elite universities feed directly into Wall Street and the City through deep, structured finance networks. ETH Zürich — and most of continental Europe — has almost none of that. We are here to change it: to give ETH's exceptional students a real path into Investment Banking, Private Equity, and the institutions that move capital.

01

Europe lags the UK & US on finance ties

Oxbridge, the Ivies, and the target-school system plug students straight into IB and PE. At ETH Zürich and across Europe, those ties are weak or non-existent — brilliant students are left without a map into high finance.

02

World-class minds, no front door

ETH Zürich is one of the top universities on earth, with extraordinary analytical talent in CS, maths, and engineering. Yet a student drawn to markets, deals, or investing often has nowhere to start. We build that front door.

03

Finance & technology, finally together

The future of IB and PE is quantitative. ETH students already have the technical edge — we pair it with real financial fluency and direct industry access, creating a profile few places in Europe can match.

Finance Education

Bringing real financial training to ETH Zürich — IB & PE fundamentals, valuation, LBOs, and deal analysis — taught student-to-student alongside the technical strengths ETH is known for.

IIUpcoming

Industry Access

The bridge Europe lacks: direct lines to banks, PE funds, and VCs. Networking events, insight sessions, and a recruiting pipeline built to put ETH Zürich on the map in high finance.

IIIUpcoming

Applied Research

Stock pitches, LBO models, case competitions, and independent market research — giving ETH students real reps on real deals before they ever interview.

IVUpcoming

Network & Mentorship

Connecting ETH students with alumni and professionals in IB and PE through a rotational Mentor-Cycle — and partner clubs across St. Gallen, LMU Munich, and the wider DACH region.

The Thesis

The world changed faster than the curriculum.

ETH Zürich attracts some of the most capable students in the world. Yet the pathways to apply that talent across finance, economics, and technology remain fragmented. We exist to close that gap — building the complete, modern financier.

01

Education vs. evolving skills

Artificial intelligence and data-driven methods are reshaping finance and consulting faster than curricula can adapt. Domain expertise alone no longer suffices — adaptability, quantitative fluency, and interdisciplinary reasoning have become the true currency.

02

Fragmented talent & access

A physicist drawn to markets, or a computer scientist fascinated by investment management, often has no clear way in. Across Switzerland and the DACH region, opportunities exist — but remain scattered across institutions, firms, and disciplines.

03

Theory without application

Brilliant minds too rarely meet real capital, real mandates, and real decisions while still at university. We bridge that distance — through research, competition, and genuine industry access.

What We Are Building

Four pillars. One standard: excellence.

Each of these programmes is upcoming — to be launched by the founding board as the club takes shape.

IUpcoming

Academic Collaboration

A cross-institutional network connecting leading universities — with initial efforts targeting ETH Zürich, the University of St. Gallen, and LMU Munich. Joint projects and events dissolve disciplinary boundaries.

IIUpcoming

Reciprocal Education

A student-led programme for the mutual exchange of knowledge. Technical students contribute mathematics, programming, and AI; finance students introduce markets, economics, and investment — through courses and workshops.

IIIUpcoming

Applied Research

Bridging theory and practice through extended case studies, trading competitions, and independent financial research. Multi-day challenges with industry partners tackle real problems in banking, asset management, and consulting.

IVUpcoming

Industry & Talent

Strategic relationships with financial institutions, consulting firms, and technology companies across the DACH region — plus a rotational Mentor-Cycle connecting members with experienced professionals.
